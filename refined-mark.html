The refinedmark.

The same faceted S, with optical corrections only.

What changed

Five corrections. The panel width, the 45° angle and the 180° turn are the original's. Units are the master's own drawing units, with a panel 1315.6 wide.

One gap value

16.3% to 18.0% of panel width

The gap is 215.0 units in the original and 236.8 in the refined mark, panel to panel and panel to crescent alike.

A slightly wider channel stays open longer as the mark gets smaller, and one value means every cut follows one rule.

Heavier crescents

48.3% to 54.0% of panel width

At their widest the crescents grow from 635.0 to 710.4 units, and their share of the ink rises from 24.7% to 26.8%.

The two Cs carry the idea, so they now carry more weight beside the panels.

The panel-end circle at exactly half the gap

106.2 to 118.4 units along the channel

In the original the circle that rounds each panel end sat 106.2 units along the channel, close to but not quite half the gap (107.5).

At exactly g/2 both panel ends are cut by the same rule, so the curves meet the channel cleanly.

A small cut under 40px

gap at 16px: 0.57px to 1.06px

Below 40px a second build opens the gap to 34.0% and the crescents to 62.0% of panel width. At 16px the crescent goes from 1.69px to 1.93px.

Under one pixel the gap fills in and the S reads as a blot; just over one pixel, it stays open.

2.5% taller

6007.2 to 6158.4 units tall

The panel width does not change. The wider gap and heavier crescents add the height, and the width to height ratio stays 0.83.

The proportions the mark is known by are kept.

Construction

One radius sets both panel ends and the crescent. Three parallel panels at 45°. The lower half is the upper half turned 180° about the centre.

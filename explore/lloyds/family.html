Proposal · The Lloyds family palette

Eight marks. One family.

Platform stays variant A. Each lab, Frontier and the two spares get their own colour, taken from the Lloyds website or the LBG theme. Panels black on white and light grounds; white on dark and coloured grounds, including Lloyds green. The colour lives only in the two Cs. A full-colour build, with no black, sits beside it.

Role by role.

The first option is the pick; the second is the choice beside it. Each sits on white, on its own dark ground and beside the Lloyds symbol as supplied, at 256, 64, 32 and 16px. At 32px and below the small cut takes over. A tonal step keeps the source hue and sets OKLCH lightness to the stated value.

Full colour.

No black anywhere. The outer panels take the role colour, the middle panel a lighter tint of it, so it is the lit focal point, and the Cs a deeper tone of the same hue. Flat fills. On dark grounds and Lloyds green the panels and middle lift so they hold. At 32px and below the small cut takes over.

The family test.

All eight picks at 32px, in role order, on white, black and Lloyds green. Black panels on white; white panels on black and on Lloyds green.

Delta E between the picks

CIEDE2000 between every pair of Cs colours. The rule is 20 or more. The light-ground Cs are above the diagonal, the dark-ground Cs below it.

In Teams

Each role's mark for dark grounds on its own dark ground, as a channel or app tile.

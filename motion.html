The mark,in motion.

Every frame is drawn from the refined geometry. The panels slide back along their own axis, and each crescent is exactly the space they leave. Pick a scene, then change the look.

One motion.

CCS moves one way: along the 45 degree axis of the panels.

In the mark, the outer panels slide back along that line and the two Cs open in the space they leave. Every scene on this page is that slide, run forwards, backwards, in a pulse or in a loop.

Interface motion uses the same move, only smaller. A section slides a short way along the axis as it comes into view. A button or link nudges 8 pixels up and to the right on hover and focus. Pages never spin or bounce; the scenes here that turn are for video, launches and loading states.

When a device asks for reduced motion, pages sit still and these scenes play only when you press play.

How CCSworks.

Four moves, one colourway per surface, two typefaces and one motion. That is the whole system, and it all comes from the mark.

The refined mark.

One gap everywhere, now 0.18 of a panel, so the S holds at small sizes and the crescents end on true curves.

Read the mark study

Four moves.

They are the whole graphic language. Nothing else decorates.

The cut

Do meet every boundary at 45 degrees, through a channel one gap wide.

Don't divide sections with a hairline or a straight edge.

Pass iton.

The step

Do step two or three lines right by one line-height each.

Don't step more than one headline on a screen.

One lit element
per surface.

The light

Do fill it with that colourway's middle-panel fade, cut at 45 degrees on its leading edge.

Don't add radial glows or light a second thing.

The found C

Do show the panels first, then let the two Cs fill the space they leave, once, at the featured moment.

Don't show a crescent on its own, or repeat it as a pattern, a bullet or a frame.

One colourway per surface.

Each surface takes its ground, a straight fade from the top-left, and one lit element in its middle-panel fade. Lab colours stay on that lab's own materials.

One motion.

Everything slides along the mark's own 45 degree axis.

  • The mark assembles once on load: the outer panels slide in along their axis and the crescents fade up in the space they leave, in about 1.2 seconds.
  • Sections slide in by 24px along the axis as they enter view.
  • List items slide 8px along the axis on hover and focus.
  • With reduced motion, everything is static and assembled.

# CCS synthesis

The refined mark sets every rule: its 45 degree axis, its gap and its lit middle panel. Each surface has one colourway and one lit element. Four moves are the whole graphic language.

## The four moves

1. **The cut.** Every section boundary is one long 45 degree run rising to the right, 96 to 400px (24vw), at the left or right edge, alternating down the page. A channel one mark gap wide, 24 to 48px, runs the whole boundary edge to edge in a contrasting ground: Stone between two dark fields, platform navy between a lab and Stone, the platform lead #5B82D1 between platform and Stone. Two surfaces with the same ground are one section, never cut.
2. **The step.** Headlines step right one line-height per line. One per screen.
3. **The light.** One lit element per surface, in the middle-panel fade, cut at 45 degrees. It holds the most important thing on that surface, such as the section headline. Where the mark leads, its middle panel is the light.
4. **The found C.** Once per page, as a reveal: the three panels in Ink on Stone with the crescent spaces empty, then the two Cs fill in the platform lead as it enters view. Filled under reduced motion. Never a crescent on its own.

One motion: a slide along the 45 degree axis.

## Type and colour

Schibsted Grotesk 800 for display. Source Serif 4 for text. Familjen Grotesk only in the CCS wordmark. IBM Plex Mono only for figures and hex. Platform fades straight from the top-left. Light surfaces are Stone #F3F1EC with Ink #0D1730. Each lab keeps its own ground, fade and lit element.

## Lab bands on the home page

The bands alternate mark left and mark right. Each ends on the cut into the next lab's ground, so they read as one relay. Under each name is a 14px label such as "Lab 1 · Ember".

The purpose lines are DRAFTS for the lab leads to confirm:

- 24x7 Services: "Round the clock, so nobody waits for the morning."
- Automated Operations: "The routine done by machines, so people can do the rest."
- Colleague Services: "Looking after the people who look after customers."
- Data & Insights: "What the numbers say, in words everyone can use."
- Frontier: "Where we try the next idea first."

## Contrast

Every text colour was checked against both ends of its ground fade. All pass WCAG AA: body text at 4.5:1 or better, large text at 3:1 or better. The tightest are the Crimson lit panel on the colourway sheet (4.9:1) and the 14px Crimson label (5.5:1).

## What each direction gave

The space between gave the cut. The hand-off gave the step and the voice. Lit from within gave the single lit element.

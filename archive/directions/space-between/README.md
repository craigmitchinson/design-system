# The space between

**The idea.** The CCS mark's two Cs, colleague and customer, are never drawn: they are the space three panels leave, so this system builds everything out of space taken from the mark.

**Type.** League Spartan Bold for display: its near-circular bowls echo the crescents,. Figtree for text: open, friendly and easy to read.

**Colour.** The six colourways stay exactly as `palette.js` defines them. Each role's deep ground fills its field; its lead colour appears once, on the mark or one action. Two neutrals join them: paper `#F2F4F7` and navy ink `#0D1730`.

**The core rule.** No shape is added to the brand; shapes are only taken out of the mark. Every cut runs at 45 degrees, the angle of the gap. A divider gap is the mark's own width (1/28 of its height, about a sixth of a panel) and is never filled: two fields meet through it and the ground shows. Pictures are cropped to the upper panel, the middle panel or a crescent, using masks cut from the mark's own paths.

**Subtly genius.** The page works the way the mark does. Nobody notices the gap is the logo, until they do.

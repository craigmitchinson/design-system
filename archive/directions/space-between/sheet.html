How the space works

One sheet for the whole system. Everything on it is cut from the mark: its panels, its two crescents, and the gap between them.

The graphic rule

We never add a shape to the brand. We only take one out of the mark, at the mark's own angle and the mark's own width.

45 degrees
Every cut, divider and section change runs at the angle of the gap between the panels. Nothing else on the page is diagonal.
One twenty-eighth
The gap is 1/28 of the mark's height, about one sixth of a panel's width. Divider gaps use the same width, square to the cut.
Whatever is behind
A gap is never filled. It shows the ground, the way the mark does. Two fields meet through it; paper never meets paper.

Ground carries the page

About four fifths of any surface is ground: the role's deep field, or paper (#F2F4F7) with navy ink (#0D1730).

Lead appears once

The lead colour sits in the mark and on one action. If it is in ten places it means nothing.

One role per surface

Labs never share a field. Where several roles appear together, each gets its own field and the gap keeps them apart.

The devices

Three devices, all cut from the mark, plus the one that costs nothing: room.

The gap divider

Section changes step at 45 degrees, and two fields meet through a channel one gap wide.

DoStep at 45 degrees, parallel edges, ground showing through.

Do notSlant at any other angle, or let the edges converge.

The crescent crop

Portraits and moments with people sit in a crescent: the C is where colleague and customer live.

DoUse the crescent exactly as cut from the mark, upright as it sits in the S.

Do notDraw a new curve, a circle or a blob because it looks friendlier.

The panel crop

Places, screens and work in progress sit in the upper panel. The middle panel is the wide crop for banners.

DoKeep the panel's 45 degree ends; let the picture fill it to the edge.

Do notRotate a panel. Its angle is the gap's angle, so it only ever sits one way.

Room

Whitespace is the fourth device. The mark is mostly space; a page should be too.

DoOne large thing, one short line, and the rest left open.

Do notFill the space the system leaves. Crowding is how the Cs disappear.

If a shape cannot be traced back to the mark, it does not belong in the brand.

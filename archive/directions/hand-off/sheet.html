One angle,one slide.

How the hand-off works. Every device on this sheet is the mark's own geometry: its angle, its gap and its slide.

The angle and the slide

Forty-five degrees, onlyRead from the master: the panels run along the unit vector (0.7071, -0.7071). Every edge, cut and step runs up and to the right at that angle, never at any other.

Gap is 0.163 of the panel215 over 1316. Wherever two fields hand off, the ground shows between them at this ratio of the field's own size.

Slide is 0.646 of the panel850 over 1316. Things move along their own axis by this much and then stop. The space they leave, shown in stone here, is where the next thing arrives.

Offsets equal in x and yLayered blocks step by the same amount across and up, so a stack of content always sits on the axis.

Type

Epilogue, one face in three weights. Black for display, Medium for leads and actions, Regular for text. Broad and blunt, so it holds its own beside full-strength colour.

The staircase: indent equals leading, so the left edge of a heading climbs at exactly the mark's angle. Use it for display and headings of two or three lines. On a phone the lines set flush.

Colour fields

One field per role, at full strength, flat. Fields meet on a hard edge or across the gap, never through a fade. The six hexes are the leads from palette.js.

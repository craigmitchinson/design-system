# The hand-off

**Idea.** In the mark, the panels slide along their own axis and each crescent is the space they leave; this direction makes that slide the whole system, so every field, block and line of type is passed along one 45-degree axis.

**Type.** Epilogue only, in Black, Medium and Regular. It is broad and blunt, so it can stand beside full-strength colour without a second face.

**Colour.** The six palette.js leads as flat, full-strength fields, one per role, on Navy #0D1730 or Stone #F3F1EC. No fades between roles: fields meet on a hard edge or across the gap.

**Core rule.** Everything comes from three numbers in the master: axis (0.7071, -0.7071), panel 1316, gap 215, slide 850. Edges run at 45 degrees only. Gaps are 0.163 of the field. Movement is a slide of 0.646 along the axis, then a stop. Offsets are equal in x and y.

**Devices.** The S field (the three panels at screen scale), the relay (lab rows whose 45-degree ends make one panel), the staircase (indent equals leading), the equal corner cut.

**Subtly genius.** The staircase. Because each line indents by exactly its own line-height, every heading's left edge climbs at the mark's angle. The type draws the mark's axis without anyone noticing a device.

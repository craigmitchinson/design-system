# Lit from within

**The idea:** the brand rule that the middle panel is always the brightest part of the mark becomes the rule for every surface: one thing is lit, everything else falls into the lab's ground.

**Type.** Schibsted Grotesk ExtraBold, the brand's named alternate, set heavy and tight. Source Serif 4 for reading keeps the voice warm. Familjen Grotesk stays on the wordmark alone. No mono labels.

**Colour.** The six colourways exactly as palette.js defines them. Each lab is an atmosphere: its ground, its ground high, its middle fade as the one light. Type is a stone tinted by the middle panel, one step below the light.

**The core device: the lit slab.** The middle panel's fade, cut on the panel's slant, with the crescent fade along its leading edge. One per surface: the action, the number or the chosen item. Grounds carry a linear fall from the top-left corner, the way every fade in the mark runs.

**Subtly genius.** The page obeys the mark's own order of light. On the home page the light follows attention: point at a row and the slab moves to it. Choose a lab and the whole page relights in its colourway.

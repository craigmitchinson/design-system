The mark.

Three panels form the S. Two crescents turn it. The mark is drawn from one gap, one radius and one half turn, so every part follows from the panel width W. The ideas behind it are in How CCS works; this page holds the rules.

What changed from v2

The gap grew from 0.163W to 0.18W and is now one value everywhere, panel to panel and panel to crescent. The crescent grew from 0.483W to 0.54W, and its tips now end on the true circle intersection instead of a clipped flat. The panel end sits exactly g/2 along the channel. The reason is small sizes: at 16px the old gap was half a pixel and closed up. W, the 45° angle, the turn centre and R = W + g/2 did not change.

Wordmark and lockups.

The wordmark is CCS in Familjen Grotesk Bold, tracked +10. Lab names are set in Familjen Medium. The mark always sits to the left of the word or above it.

40px Smallest full build.

16px Smallest small cut. Use it for favicons, avatars and tabs.

Which version, where.

Primary

Dark screens: slides, app splash, hero.

Light ground

Documents, email, web on Stone or white.

Print on light

Paper, canvas, embroidery: one ink, the print ink of the colourway.

Small size

Under 40px: favicons, Teams, tabs. The small cut.

Mono

Photography, foil, single-colour print. White on dark, Ink on light.

The glow rule. Glow belongs to dark grounds only, and only from 64px tall. Its radius is 3% of the mark's height up to 200px, then 2%, and never more than 16px. Above 200px its strength eases down to 60%. No glow on light grounds, in print, on merchandise or on the small cut.

Do and don't.

Clear space. Keep half the mark's width clear on every side.

Do

Don't

The Frontier F.

Frontier, the innovation forum, uses its own F. It is the same family: the same panel width, the same gap and crescent, the same fades. The four labs always use the S.

The same rules hold for the F: clear space of half its width, glow on dark from 64px, the small cut under 40px, never rotated or recoloured.
